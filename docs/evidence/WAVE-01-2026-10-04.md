Clinical content is rejected in the shared Patient master; contextual records are subsequent work.

## Limits

This closes security primitives, not all historical security capabilities across future features. Contextual/private notes, appointment/report joins, print/email/attachments, scheduling concurrency, UI ergonomics and broader load/deadlock tests remain in subsequent waves. Raw SQL/get_all are privileged internal primitives; future services must call explicit guards. Audit is application-level append-only, not protection against a privileged database administrator. HTTP fixtures use exact synthetic teardown outside the operational retention path; the helper is not a public API. No real patient data, provider credentials, SdI/TS traffic or production access.
