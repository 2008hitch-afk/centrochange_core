## Native bank truth

A guarded management service configures a native Bank Account/bank GL account for the center. Bounded statement movements create native submitted Bank Transactions, retaining immutable source fields and identity/content digests. Reimport replays the same movement; changed content under the same identity fails. Import produces no Payment Entry or GL posting.

Own-center invoice candidates are deterministic: unmatched, one candidate or ambiguous, with exact invoice-reference narrowing. Human selection creates a native Payment Entry through WP-02, posts to the configured bank GL and uses native bank allocation/clearance. Replay returns the same reconciliation; a different invoice cannot consume a reconciled movement. Native bank cancellation cancels its dedicated receipt and restores native outstanding balance, preserving the source/history and audited reason. Withdrawals are retained as unresolved native movements; linking payables is integrated in WP-04.

## Terms, block and override

Payment agreements are append-only and prospective. Their mode/agreed date is frozen on booking and carried into the immutable economic source; changing future terms cannot silently defer an existing unbilled debt. Unbilled closed Due/Unpaid sessions use their historical terms; after native invoice posting, its outstanding balance and due date are authoritative. Package-covered/non-due/waived/zero-value facts do not become an unbilled debt.

Booking/rescheduling and performing an already-booked subsequent session enforce the previous-debt rule server-side. Changing center cannot bypass a shared Patient hold; professionals receive a generic payment-review error, without foreign-center amounts or references. Financial position/drill-down remains center-scoped. Explicit authorized overrides require actor, reason and new due date, retain the original native balance/due history and expire automatically. Idempotent replay binds the payload. Current-read locks cover critical native financial facts under MariaDB REPEATABLE READ.

The payment-block notice contains amount/currency/deadline and DEV payment instructions. Internal delivery reuses the native Notification Log outbox. Patient email is **SIMULATED**, retained as a local native Communication linked to a scoped immutable notice; no Email Queue, SMTP, provider or fabricated delivery. Missing email is explicit. Notice/retry dedupes; native communication direct/list and manual-share paths cannot leak the financial body to professionals. Real email/provider configuration belongs to later gates.
