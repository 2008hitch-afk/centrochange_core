## Product and supported implementation

`/centrochange` is the authenticated primary operational web app required by HC-0005 DECISION-004: agenda, scoped booking/reschedule/cancel, patient history, separate organizational/private notes and revisions, closure/payment/reopen/waiver controls, recurring availability/absence, operational reports and notifications. `/primo_incontro` exposes fixed Marco/Francesca first-meeting slots/form without arbitrary patient/practitioner selection; public branded website remains deferred. Native Frappe routing/assets/authentication, guarded app APIs and ERPNext/Healthcare masters stay authoritative. Desk is the administration fallback.

Internal outbox delivery uses native Notification Log with a unique source reference, preserved attempt count, bounded automatic retries, visible failure and authorized audited retry. No external mail account/provider is configured. Native scheduler invokes catch-up and dispatch; actual short-worker delivery is tested. This proves internal notification reliability, not delivery through a real email provider.

Bounded report/CSV/print projections apply the same patient/center/professional authorization as operational APIs. CSV neutralizes spreadsheet formulas; print contains only explicitly selected operational fields; management aggregates contain no patient identity or clinical/private body and separate currencies. Truncation is explicit and incomplete exports are refused. Native report/export bypass denials remain in the HTTP suite. Clinical requests redact body/request/locals from persisted Error Log and native logger records using supported model/logging extensions.

Booking-date Item Price policy remains canonical and immutable. DST fold/gap/cross-offset intervals are rejected rather than create unrepresentable native local-time appointments. MariaDB deadlock rollback resets callbacks and preserves the real retryable cause after the database discards savepoints; the committed operation remains idempotent on caller retry.
