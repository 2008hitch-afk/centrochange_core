Issue #37; parent GOAL-01 #22; stacked after Wave 4 #36. **PASS for WP-01 tested foundation**, not a full Finance/Admin or goal-completion claim. Pinned WP-00 DEV runtime with versioned source overlay; no production/provider access.

## Implementation

Native Patient/Customer and Healthcare Practitioner/Supplier fields remain the accounting mappings. Center accounting uses a native leaf Cost Center linked to Branch and Company, preserving the shared platform and canonical session. Guarded mapping services support idempotent creation/replay and audited correction; generic Patient/Practitioner/Branch mapping edits are denied. Existing explicit native fiscal-subject mappings retain native semantics; no unapproved one-to-one payer/supplier business rule is imposed.

CC Economic Source Link represents the distinct immutable trace from canonical Patient Appointment to native Customer/Supplier/Item/Company/Cost Center and the original booking-date service/price/duration/currency snapshot. A correction creates a new source trace; the previous subject/snapshot remains reconstructable. This is not a second Patient/Session or accounting ledger. Capture/master mapping never creates Sales Invoice, Purchase Invoice, Payment Entry or GL postings automatically.

CC Finance and read-only CC Accountant provide a scoped DEV accounting seam. Professional center-wide financial reads/actions are denied. Accounting roles receive no Patient clinical-master or private-note access. Native Customer/Supplier/Cost Center list/direct lookups and source projections are scoped by current finance center membership; manual shares cannot bypass scope. Clinical/private content does not enter source traces. CC Finance Settings keeps the unpaid-session compensation policy UNSET and fiscal providers simulation-only; disabling the latter is denied server-side.
