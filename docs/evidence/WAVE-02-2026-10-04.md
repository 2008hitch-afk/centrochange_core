## New material business dependency: snapshot price date

TD-024 states that the capture-date policy is separate. TD-025 requires an immutable historical snapshot but does not specify which date selects the effective Item Price. Canonical testlab `evidence/CAP011-013-CATALOG-PRICING-SNAPSHOT.md` explicitly leaves **booking date versus session date** to downstream decision. HC-0005 DECISION-008 and the executable baseline require historical immutability, but do not settle this choice.

These alternatives produce different prices when a future tariff begins between booking and performance. A technical default would become an unapproved business rule. Requested decision: use booking date or scheduled session date to resolve the tariff, then freeze it when booking. Recommendation for review: scheduled session date; no policy selected or implemented.
