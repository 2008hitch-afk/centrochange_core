# WP-06 — scoped finance reporting and workspace

Issue #46; GOAL-01 #22; stack after WP-05 #47. **PASS for tested DEV reporting/workspace scope.** Final adapter and immutable-image integrated verification remain subsequent packages.

Cancelled documents are excluded and submitted returns retain their signed native facts. Every total includes its composing document drill-down. Accounting uses posting dates; operational sessions use session dates. Customer totals follow the shared fiscal payer; supplier totals follow the fiscal supplier. Professional invoices are not silently allocated across people sharing a supplier. No invented margin or unknown compensation total: explicit null/REVIEW where definitions or snapshots are missing. Every service enforces server-side scope independently of controls. The operational workspace links authorized users to finance; finance access cannot grant clinical access.
