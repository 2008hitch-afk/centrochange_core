## Implementation

Native Patient Appointment remains the canonical session. Custom fields hold immutable booking snapshots and controlled closure/economic state. CC Settlement records append-only operational payment facts; they never create accounting postings automatically. Package issuance, reservation, consumption, release, correction and adjustment use an append-only ledger with derived balances and atomic, idempotent services. Package centers/restrictions are explicit configuration.

Organizational and confidential professional notes use separate DocTypes. Revisions retain the original. Private bodies do not enter management audit or native Version. Current professional identity, exact patient/center assignment and membership are required, including list and direct serialization. Administrator/Management/Secretariat receive no application-level private-note bypass; database/root administration is outside that trust boundary.

Cancellation uses elapsed UTC time against Europe/Rome session start: exactly 48 hours is timely; even one microsecond below is late. Authorized waiver retains the late fact and appends a corrective package event. Performed closure, settlement correction and authorized reopen are audited; 21:00 reminders and 07:00 next-day locking support idempotent catch-up. Lock cutoff is also enforced synchronously if the scheduler is delayed. Ambiguous/nonexistent local timestamps fail explicitly.

Patient history and agenda are bounded projections with explicit context checks. Queries use exact patient/center pairs, avoiding cross-center leakage when shared patient identity remains accessible elsewhere. Mutation authorization and absence/overlap reads use current locked reads under the pinned MariaDB REPEATABLE READ isolation.
